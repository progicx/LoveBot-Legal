# LoveBot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how LoveBot ("LoveBot", "the Bot", "we", "us") handles information when used on Discord.

LoveBot is operated by **Progic Productions**.

## 1. Information LoveBot May Process

LoveBot may process limited Discord-related information necessary to provide its features, including:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server/guild IDs
- Discord role information
- Join timestamps
- Account creation timestamps
- Verification-related role status
- Moderation and AntiRaid events
- Bot command interactions
- Server configuration relevant to LoveBot's features

LoveBot does not require users to provide passwords, payment information, or other sensitive account credentials.

## 2. Why We Process This Information

LoveBot processes this information to provide and operate its features, including:

- AntiRaid and anti-abuse protection
- Verification enforcement
- Moderation-related actions
- Logging of security events
- Slash command functionality
- Fun and entertainment commands
- Bot configuration and operational reliability

## 3. Data Storage

LoveBot is designed to store as little information as reasonably necessary.

Certain configuration and AntiRaid state information may be stored persistently so that the Bot can continue operating correctly after restarts or redeployments.

Security and moderation events may also be logged in private Discord channels configured by the server owner.

LoveBot does not create public logs of AntiRaid actions.

## 4. Hosting and Infrastructure

LoveBot is currently hosted using **Railway**.

As part of normal operation, technical information may be processed by infrastructure providers where necessary to host and operate the Bot.

## 5. Data Sharing

LoveBot does not sell personal information.

We do not share user data for advertising purposes.

Information may only be disclosed where:

- necessary to operate the Bot,
- required by law,
- necessary to protect LoveBot, its users, or Discord communities from abuse or security threats.

## 6. Discord

LoveBot operates through Discord and is therefore also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Discord may independently collect or process information when users interact with Discord services.

## 7. Data Retention

LoveBot retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for operation, security, moderation, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.

Temporary operational data may be discarded automatically.

Persistent configuration or security-related state may remain stored until it is no longer needed or is manually removed.

## 8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect LoveBot and its configuration.

Sensitive credentials such as Discord bot tokens and infrastructure secrets are not intentionally exposed publicly.

No system can guarantee absolute security, and users acknowledge that online services may be subject to technical failures or abuse attempts.

## 9. Children's Privacy

LoveBot is intended for use through Discord and is not designed to independently collect personal information from children.

Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.
